# Detours story bank

The only personal facts a Detours post may state. Every line here is either on Tony's LinkedIn
profile or was confirmed by Tony in writing (Nov 13, 2026). If a personal claim is not in this file,
it does not go in a post. Opinions and lessons are fine when they are framed as Tony's view
("my read", "what I'd tell you"), never as events that happened.

Not in this file means not in a post: other employers, family, relationships, health, money,
funding, revenue, user numbers, internal product decisions, anything said in private.

- C3. By Roadr he was building in Python and SQL every week.

- C6. The Roadr consulting engagement wrapped at the end of 2024. He could look for the next HR role or build the thing he kept wishing existed. He chose to build.
