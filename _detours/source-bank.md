# Detours source bank

The standing pool of published figures Detours can draw on, so a post never has to lean on Tony's
own story for its substance. The weekly research brief (`_detours/research/`) still supplies the
freshest figures for each topic; this bank is the shelf behind it.

How to use it:
- Every figure here was found on the source's own page or in its press release (Nov 19, 2026).
  The drafter and the second reviewer still open the URL and confirm the exact figure before use
  (RULES.md fact rule 3). Entries marked `verify:` need an extra check named on the line.
- Prefer the primary URL. Where only coverage was reachable, the line says "via".
- Pair a scary headline with the data that puts it in context. Each pillar lists pairs.
- Reuse limits: a source may be the lead figure of a post once every 90 days; log it in
  `used:` on its line (e.g. `used: No. 5 lead`). A figure already used in a post is marked below.
- News ages fast: news figures are "context only" after 90 days unless a newer edition replaces them.
- The research run adds new entries at the bottom of the right pillar and never deletes old ones.
- The LinkedIn engine's evidence corpus (83 verified hiring and AI sources in the
  multi-bot-orchestration worker, last regenerated Aug 10, 2026) is a second shelf. Ask for it by
  topic; do not copy it here.
