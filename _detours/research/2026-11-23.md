- 121,401 job cuts cited AI so far in 2026, about 20% of 617,189 total announced cuts; Market/Economic Conditions (130,531) has now overtaken AI as the leading reason for 2026; AI was cited for 1,265 cuts in October, its lowest monthly total this year; total cuts through October are down 44% from 1,099,500 in the same period of 2025 | Announced US job cuts by stated reason, Challenger, Gray & Christmas October 2026 report, released Nov 5, 2026, January to October 2026. October 2026 alone: +165,000 jobs, unemployment rate 4.1%. BLS, The Economics Daily, Nov 18, 2026 (most recent two months preliminary), and Employment Situation release, Nov 6, 2026 | https://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2026/payroll-employment-increased-by-885000-from-october-2025-to-october-2026.htm | checked on the page: yes
- No clear AI footprint yet | The occupational mix is not yet changing in ways that clearly line up with AI, and measures of AI usage show no connection to changes in employment or unemployment.
