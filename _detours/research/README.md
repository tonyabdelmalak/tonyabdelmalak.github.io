# Detours research briefs

One file per week, written by the Monday research run: `YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
Front matter `reading:` (2 or 3 items) becomes the "Worth reading" section of that week's email.

    ---
    date: 2026-11-16
    topic: <the queue topic this brief supports>
    reading:
      - title: <article or study title>
        source: <publisher>
        url: <final URL>
        take: <one plain sentence in Tony's voice on why it's worth reading>
    ---
    ## Verified figures
    - <figure> | <what it measures, who, when> | <final URL> | checked on the page: yes

Rules: public, credible, linkable sources only. Recent: news within 90 days, research within 2 years.
"What people say behind closed doors" means published executive surveys, earnings calls, on-record
interviews and published research, never leaks, rumors or anonymous posts. Paraphrase; no quote longer than 15 words.
