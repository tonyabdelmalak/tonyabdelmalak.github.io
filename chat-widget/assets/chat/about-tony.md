## Products I designed and built

### ReflectivAI (reflectiv-ai.com)
AI role-play and coaching for life-sciences reps and commercial teams. Adaptive AI physicians that push back in real time, coaching built on Signal Intelligence (an eight-dimension framework I co-developed with Desiree Young), and agents that turn a territory report into a plan for the week and a pre-call brief. Web platform, Role Play Simulator and iOS app. Started Jan 2025.

Want to hear what made me build it?

---

### CareerPaver (careerpaverai.com)
For job seekers from analyst to VP. Reads a posting the way a screener does, shows what's costing you the interview, tailors without inventing experience, then gets you ready for the conversation: the Pressure Room replays your interview answers back to you (pace, filler words, pauses), and you can rehearse salary conversations. Agents rank new roles and line up next steps; nothing happens until you approve it. Start with the free Debrief. Web platform and iOS app. Started Jan 2026.

This site used to have early interview-practice and resume demos. Those ideas grew into CareerPaver, a separate and much fuller product. The old demos are gone.

---

### Lumira (lumiraconnect.com)
A private space for any two people who matter to each other, with Mira, an AI coach grounded in relationship science. Guided check-ins, repair after a hard moment, rehearsal for difficult conversations, and a daily question that stays sealed until both people answer. Built end to end. Started Jan 2026.
