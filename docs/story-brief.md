## Who I am, in one line

Founder and architect of ReflectivAI, CareerPaver and Lumira. I build AI practice and coaching for the
conversations that matter: sales calls, job interviews and relationships. Based in Los Angeles.

## The throughline

1. **Pharma sales (Sepracor, 2007 to 2009).** I called on physicians. The only place to practice a hard
   conversation was in front of the physician.
2. **Recruiting and HR, more than a decade (2009 to 2024).** NBCUniversal, HBO, Sony Pictures, Quibi, then
   Flowserve and Roadr. I sat on the hiring side of the table and saw why strong candidates never hear back.
   I also learned that the working relationships that last are the ones where someone notices the friction
   early and says something.
3. **The connection.** Across both careers, a lot turned on a single conversation: an interview, a meeting
   with a physician, a salary negotiation. Researching what to say and practicing saying it are two
   different things.
4. **Now.** I taught myself to build with AI and designed and built three products, each one drawn from a
   chapter of that career, so people can practice before those conversations happen.
