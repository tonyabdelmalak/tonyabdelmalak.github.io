About

A lot can turn on one conversation.

I started my career selling pharmaceuticals to physicians. Then I moved into recruiting and talent acquisition, spending more than a decade at NBCUniversal, HBO, Sony Pictures and Quibi.

Across both careers, I saw how much can turn on a conversation. An interview. A meeting with a physician. A salary negotiation. Preparation matters, but researching what to say and practicing saying it are two different things.

So I taught myself to build with AI and founded ReflectivAI, CareerPaver and Lumira to give people more chances to practice before those conversations happen.

  1. Pharma sales → ReflectivAI

    The only place to practice a hard conversation was in front of the physician. Now reps can practice with an AI physician that pushes back.

  2. Recruiting → CareerPaver

    I was the recruiter on the other side of the table, so I know why strong candidates never hear back.

  3. HR → Lumira

    The relationships that last are the ones where someone notices the friction early and says something.

What I’ve built

Three products, one idea: practice first.

Each one comes from a chapter of my career. I designed and built all three, end to end.

Life-sciences sales · since 2025

ReflectivAI

AI role-play and coaching for reps and commercial teams in pharma, medical device and biotech. Adaptive AI physicians push back in real time, coaching is built on Signal Intelligence, the eight-dimension framework I co-developed with Desiree Young, and agents turn a territory report into a plan for the week and a pre-call brief.

I designed and built the web platform, the Role Play Simulator and the iOS app.

Visit ReflectivAI
Job seekers, analyst to VP · since 2026

CareerPaver

It reads a posting the way a screener does, shows what’s costing you the interview, and tailors without inventing experience. Then it gets you ready for the conversation itself, with interview rehearsal in the Pressure Room and salary practice. Agents rank new roles and line up next steps, and nothing happens until you approve it.

Looking for the AI Interview Practice demo or the Resume Optimizer that used to live here? Those ideas grew into CareerPaver. Start with the free Debrief.

Visit CareerPaver
Any two people · since 2026

Lumira

A private space for any two people who matter to each other: partners, family, friends or colleagues. Mira, an AI coach grounded in relationship science, helps each person say the hard thing before it turns into a fight, with guided check-ins, repair after a hard moment, rehearsal for difficult conversations and a daily question that stays sealed until both have answered.

I designed and built it end to end.
