Workforce Planning & Forecast

If attrition runs a little hotter or hiring a little slower, what does headcount look like a year out? Nine scenarios per department, from today’s numbers.

Headcount scenarios

Projected headcount by scenario

On this sample, every scenario shrinks headcount over 12 months because monthly exits outpace hires at current rates. The attrition and hiring mix decides by how much: cutting attrition 20% and hiring 20% faster ends 43% to 69% above the current-rates path, depending on the department.
